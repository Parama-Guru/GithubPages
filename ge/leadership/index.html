Roles

Responsibilities & authorities

ProprietorLeadership

Responsibilities

  • Strategic business planning
  • Policy decisions
  • Provide adequate resources

Authorities

  • Authority to approve decisions, hire and fire
  • HOD's
Head ProjectsProject Management

Responsibilities

  • Interact with customer to get project requirements
  • Plan execution based on requirements and time schedules
  • Effective utilization of manpower and machines
  • Simulate & evaluate contingency plan
  • Review Project status and address bottle necks & issues
  • Ensure on time project handover

Authorities

  • Authority to escalate issues
  • Authority to approve changes in project activities.
Technical headElectrical design

Responsibilities

  • Identify requirements (customer, statutory, legal..)and ensure electrical design meet the same
  • Interact with customer to understand the requirements
  • Participate in project feasibility analysis
  • Implement quality controls to ensure compliance with customer requirements
  • Design and validate before handover to customer
  • Prepare schedule and monitor progress of projects
  • Co-ordinate with project team and ensure successful project completion
  • Maintain design records

Authorities

  • Authority to approve design change
  • Authority to be single point of contact for projects
PurchaseMaterial purchase

Responsibilities

  • Identify & evaluate suppliers / sub contractors for material, special process and services
  • Negotiate cost and delivery terms with existing and new supplier
  • Release P.O and follow up material delivery
  • Audit existing and supplier
  • Evaluate supplier performance and interact for improvement.

Authorities

  • Authority to disqualify a supplier / sub contractor
Safety EngineerSafety

Responsibilities

  • Provide health and safety measures
  • Identification of environmental aspects and determining the project objectives and target
  • Identify and evaluate the unsafe environment and practices
  • Identify the root causes of safety and industrial hygiene
  • Develop hazard control processes, methods and programs
  • Recommend solutions to issues that need improvement
  • Advise several ways of controlling potential threats
  • Monitor implementation of safety plans according to plans and objectives
  • Monitor and track safety systems by using computer systems and spreadsheets
  • Communicate plans and strategies across the entire business unit from time to time
  • Maintain and manage equipment and processes related to each project of health and safety
  • Suggest several preventive methods and practices like recycling, waste disposal, pollution control, etc.
  • Collaborate with other levels of engineering staff, quality assurance, quality improvement, regulatory compliance specialists, etc.
  • Measure and audit the effectiveness of hazard control programs
  • Prepare future safety plans based on past experiences and facts

Authorities

  • Authority to stop any work in the event of potential serious violation.
  • Authority to communicate with customer regarding safety issues
Quality EngineerQuality assurance

Responsibilities

  • Inspect all incoming material, job work & special process material
  • Maintain incoming inspection records
  • Inspect site work task against MS & customer, statutory requirements.
  • Maintain site work inspection records
  • Inspect finished work as per client requirement
  • Maintain master list of measuring and monitoring devices
  • Ensure all measuring and monitoring devices are calibrated
  • Interact with customer regarding quality requirements and any issues
  • Register customer / site complaints and initiate CAPA

Authorities

  • Authority to block suspected lots and escalate
  • Authority to recommend supplier disqualification
  • Stop site work if MS & other requirements are not met.
  • Authority to block/discard any measuring equipment if found defective.
  • Authority to communicate with customer regarding quality issues
