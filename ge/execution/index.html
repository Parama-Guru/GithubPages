How we work

How we work

We are authorized to carryout all electrical works with no limitation in type of voltage or capacity. We have executed turnkey projects in various categories based on Voltage level 230KV, 110KV, 33kV & 11KV.

Handling local issues

Rapport on the ground

Ganesh Electricals has rich experience executing projects in Pillaipakkam, Oragadam, Vallam vadagal and neighboring area. We've good public, industrial relation and good rapport with local and district administration authorities.

We don't entertain any issues affecting project work. In case of any unforeseen issues, it's our responsibility to resolve it amicably without disturbing our client.

TNEB liaisoning

HT service in 211 days

  1. Collections of documents as per TNEB check list from the client.10 days
  2. Getting Attestation from Notary Public of all documents.10 days
  3. Submitting the Filled up HT Application to TNEB10 days
  4. Following the Application and getting EMD receipt from TNEB50 days
  5. Collecting the DD for EMD from Client and paying to TNEB10 days
  6. Getting Development charges Advice from TNEB50 days
  7. Collecting the DD for Development charges from Client and paying to TNEB10 days
  8. Coordinating with TNEB peoples for completion of TNEB portion of Works.55 days

Total211 days
