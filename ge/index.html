Quality & Safety

Quality & Safety

Quality Policy

Ganesh Electricals is committed to consistently achieve client satisfaction by providing all EHV and LV Electrical related services including design on turnkey basis and assuring timely completion of projects within budget and with excellent quality through employee involvement and team work

Our mission is to continually improve and integrate systems for Safety, Health, Environmental, Personnel, Industrial Relations and Quality Assurance, to ensure consistent and effective utilization of resources.

Quality Standards

Work on a work task starts only when conditions do not adversely impact quality, comply with government regulations, contract technical specifications, industry standards, or product installation instructions.The Quality Manager identifies supplemental start-work requirements that apply to a specific project when they are necessary to assure quality results.

Completed work is protected from damage as specified by government regulations, contract technical specifications, industry standards or product installations instructions.The Quality Manager identifies supplemental protections that apply to specific project when they are necessary to assure quality results .

EHS Policy

At Ganesh Electricals ,It is the Policy to conduct Corporate Engineering, Construction and maintenance operations in a safe and Environmentally responsible manner. Health, Safety and Environmental Protection are the responsibility of every employee.

We are Committed to:

  1. Hold the interest of Every affected personnel and our client paramount
  2. Complying with and maintain environmental ,health and safety practices that meet applicable legal and other requirements
  3. Avoid environmental Pollution
  4. Reducing, Reusing and/or recycling our available materials and waste
  5. Preventing unacceptable risk in our operations and providing an injury and occupational illness free work environment
  6. Provide adequate and sufficient resources to achieve an injury and occupational illness free work environment.
  7. Fostering a culture of EHS awareness among all affected personnel in our organization, business partners ,client and the general public
  8. Continually improving health ,safety and environmental protection performance
  9. Promoting an environment of learning and a culture of continual improvement

Each person has the responsibility to support the above directives by his / her professionalism
