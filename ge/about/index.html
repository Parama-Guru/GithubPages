About

Ganesh Electricals

GANESH ELECTRICALS, an electrical engineering and contracting company established in 1997 to provide quality, safety, reliability and timely completion of projects. GANESH ELECTRICALS has successfully managed to carve its niche in the field of electrical engineering and contracting in India.

We wish to introduce ourselves as one of the prosperous electrical contractors in Chennai carrying out electrical design & installation on turnkey basis for valuable clients from 230KV, 110kV EHV substations to 33KV, 11kV Installation, up to downstream electrical works including plant, machinery and office electrification works.

We are authorized to carryout all electrical works with no limitation in type of voltage or capacity. We have executed turnkey projects in various categories based on Voltage level 230KV, 110KV, 33kV & 11KV.

Our purpose

To undertake specialized turnkey industrial electrification projects and power the industrial sector of our nation.

Founded in 1997

Ganesh Electricals was established in 1997 by Mr. B.Kumareson to further enhance and capitalise on his strong background and knowledge of the Power Distribution and Transmission sector.
