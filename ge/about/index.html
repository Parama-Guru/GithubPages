About

Powering Infrastructure. Engineering Reliability. Building Trust.

Established in 1997, Ganesh Electricals is a trusted name in electrical engineering and contracting, delivering high-performance electrical infrastructure solutions across industrial and commercial sectors.

What we do

Our expertise

  • Turnkey electrical projects
  • 230 kV and 110 kV EHV substations
  • 33 kV and 11 kV substations
  • Electrical distribution networks
  • Industrial electrification
  • Commercial infrastructure

At Ganesh Electricals, we don't just build electrical infrastructure – we power progress, strengthen industries and create lasting partnerships.

We are authorized to carry out all electrical works with no limitation in type of voltage or capacity. We have executed turnkey projects in various categories based on voltage level: 230 kV, 110 kV, 33 kV and 11 kV.

Our purpose

To undertake specialized turnkey industrial electrification projects and power the industrial sector of our nation.

Founded in 1997

Ganesh Electricals was established in 1997 by Mr. B. Kumareson to further enhance and capitalise on his strong background and knowledge of the Power Distribution and Transmission sector. Incorporated / registered on 06/05/1997.

Credentials

Licence, registration & rating

ESA License

Udyam (MSME) Registration

Financial stability
