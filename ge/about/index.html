About

Ganesh Electricals

Welcome to Ganesh Electricals! We are a leading ESA grade electrical contractor in Tamil Nadu. Established in the year 1997, we have been a reliable presence for more than a decade and have deep expertise in executing E.H.V and M.V/L.V installations for a wide spectrum of industries (automobile, garment, plastic/leather, software and multistoried buildings).
